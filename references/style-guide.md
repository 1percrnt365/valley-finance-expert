# Valley AI Financial Writing Style Guide

## 1. Core Principles: Evidence-Based Analysis
Every claim must be backed by objective data.
- **Data over Opinion**: Use financial statements, TAM (Total Addressable Market), penetration rates, and growth percentages.
- **Ticker-Centric**: Always use tickers for stocks (e.g., $TSLA, $NVDA, $AAPL).

## 2. Balanced Perspective: Bull vs. Bear
A professional report must show both sides of the coin.
- **Bull Case**: Why the price will go up. Focus on growth drivers and competitive advantages.
- **Bear Case**: Why the price might drop. Focus on risks, competition, and macroeconomic factors.
- **Probability Thinking**: Assign estimated probabilities to outcomes (e.g., "Bull Case: 60%, Bear Case: 40%").

## 3. Direct and Provocative Headlines
Use titles that spark curiosity or state a strong thesis.
- Example: "Is the AI Bubble Actually Real? Let's Look at the Data."
- Example: "Why $LLY is the Only Winner in the GLP-1 Race."

## 4. Community Engagement
Always end with a call to action for feedback.
- Example: "What do you think? Share your thoughts below!"
- Example: "Let's discuss this in the comments."

## 5. Length Guidance
- **Quick Brief**: 300-500 characters.
- **Deep Analysis**: 500-1000+ characters.
