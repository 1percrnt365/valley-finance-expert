# Valley AI Financial Report Template

## Title: [Provocative or Thesis-Driven Headline]
**Report Date: [YYYY-MM-DD]**

### 1. Introduction: Market Context & Thesis
- **Brief Overview**: 1-2 paragraphs introducing the topic or stock.
- **Current Diagnosis**: Briefly state the current market sentiment and your core thesis.

### 2. Analysis: The Dual Perspective (Bull vs. Bear)
#### 📈 Bull Case (The Growth Driver)
- **Point 1**: Data-backed reason for growth (e.g., TAM, Revenue growth).
- **Point 2**: Competitive advantage (Moat) or unique catalyst.
- **Evidence**: Quote news, expert opinions, or specific financial figures (e.g., "$50B target by 2026").

#### 📉 Bear Case (The Risk Factor)
- **Point 1**: Counter-argument or potential risk (e.g., Saturation, Macro uncertainty).
- **Point 2**: Regulatory risks or interest rate impact.
- **Evidence**: Provide data that contradicts the bull case for balance.

### 3. Conclusion: Action Plan & Strategy
- **Investment Implication**: Summary of the analysis.
- **Correlation**: Mention correlation with other assets (e.g., "+0.7 correlation with BTC").
- **Portfolio Strategy**: Specific buy/sell/hold opinions, weight in portfolio (e.g., "10% weight"), and RSI/technical buy points.
- **Stop-Loss/Risk Management**: Explicit risk mitigation steps (e.g., "10% trailing stop-loss").

### 4. Community Feedback
- "What's your take? Let's discuss in the comments!"
- "Any different views? Feel free to share."
